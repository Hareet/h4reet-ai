## Decision

1. **No usage event, no span, no panel for three behaviours.** The dinosaur's eating (spec 024), the twin's and the dog's run from the dinosaur (spec 025), and the twin's petting of the dog (spec 026) send no usage event, open no request, write no span, and add no dashboard panel or count. `EVENT_KINDS`, `config/room-events.yaml`, and row V4 stay as they are for them; row V4's "Never carries" keeps "avatar" in its words.
2. **It is a deviation, and permanent for these three features.** Principle II's "telemetry that makes it observable in production" and Development Workflow 7's "A feature without a graph is not done" are not met for these behaviours. Each spec carries the written justification the governance asks for (024's A5, 025's "Deviations named", 026's "Deviation named"); this ADR is the governance's "ADR if permanent". No accepted ADR is reversed (each spec's words: "No accepted ADR is reversed").

## Alternatives considered

Each from the questions' "Others", in their words:

- **Send the event, and amend row V4** (Q116: "a once-a-visit `chase_started` event with no identifier, row V4's "never carries" amended in your words to say it shows the visitor was the dinosaur, a line in `config/room-events.yaml`, and a use panel"; Q117: "both send their event, with row V4 amended in your words"; spec 024's first rework had taken `eat_started`, its Q108). Gives Principle II's telemetry and Workflow 7's graph; changes a published promise about what leaves a visitor's device, which only the Author's words can do. Rejected provisionally.
- **An event for every visit that walked, with a yes/no** (Q116's second other): "which carries the same". Rejected provisionally.
- **For the petting, count only `done` and `skipped`, never `cut`** (Q142: "a once-a-visit event counting pettings `done` and `skipped` only (never `cut`), with a use panel and a line in `config/room-events.yaml`"). Rejected provisionally (Q142's recommended answer).
- **Leave the petting unobserved without naming it** (Q142). The governance asks a written justification. Rejected.
- **No ADR, each spec's justification alone** (Q149, Q152, Q161), reading "if permanent" as "until a later spec adds telemetry" (spec 024's Q-R2c, `spec.md:41`). Rejected provisionally: confirmed, the deviation is permanent for each feature (specs 024 and 025: "if he confirms it, it is permanent for this feature"; spec 026: "permanent for this feature if he confirms it").
- **An ADR per spec** (Q149's and Q161's others). The reason is one, so the record is one. Rejected provisionally.

## Consequences

- In production, nobody can see how often a visitor eats, runs the twin and the dog off, or sees the petting, or whether any of these misbehaves short of a failed model file. A failure that doesn't fail a file shows only in the visitor's console (spec 024's one `console.warn`) or in a draft build's frame log (specs 025 and 026), neither of which leaves the device. These behaviours are proved by their tests, not by production telemetry.
- The figures they depend on are observed: the dinosaur's and the room's dog's model failures count in `avatar_failed`, and its panel's description names the dog's files (spec 025, FR-026).
- Row V4 keeps its words, and the disclosure needs no change for these three features.
- A review of specs 024, 025, or 026 against Principle II and Workflow 7 finds the deviation named in the spec and recorded here.

**What would reverse it.** Any one of these, by a new ADR that supersedes this one (ADRs are "immutable once accepted", `docs/adr/README.md`):

- The Author overturns Q116, Q117, or Q142 on the page, taking the event: then, in each spec's words, "the event, its row amendment in his words, and its panel come with it" (spec 025, `spec.md:239`; spec 024, `spec.md:184`: "the event, row V4's amendment in his words, its `config/room-events.yaml` line, and its panel"; spec 026, `spec.md:231`: "the event, its line in `config/room-events.yaml`, and its panel").
- Row V4's "Never carries" changes, in his words, so that the avatar may be carried.
- The trigger stops being the dinosaur's alone (for example, if another avatar could eat or set off the run), so that an event's kind no longer says which avatar the visitor chose.
