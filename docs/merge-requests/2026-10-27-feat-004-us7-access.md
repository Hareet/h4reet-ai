## What changed and why

User Story 7: every visitor reaches every piece. The branch has 5 commits on 7a4d5ee before this note: 16 files, 1,189 insertions and 93 deletions. Every file is under `apps/web/` except plan.md and tasks.md.

- **"Around the room" in its place** (`src/room/room.css`, `src/room/text-copy.ts`, T171). The portrait grid's areas run toolbar, stage, list, talk, around, resume. In landscape, "around" spans both columns before the resume. The section is a Tab stop of its own (`tabindex="0"`), named by its heading, after "Hold to talk" (Amendment B8's Tab order), with the room's focus ring. It holds no control, so this is how a keyboard reaches it. It updates in place, in no live region.
- **The portrait reserve** (`room.css`, `src/room/compose/stages.ts`, T171 and the review's B1). Both bounds are written in `max-width` (34.99rem, 24.99rem). `compose/stages.ts` follows the rule (`ROOM_CSS.reserveMaxWidthRem`, `narrowReserveRem`), so every check at the supported stages runs at the stage a visitor sees.
- **The list** (`src/room/a11y.ts`) needed no change. Its buttons already carried the name, a visually hidden ": ", and the topic's phrase or the resume action's UI word (T105). T169 holds it.
- **The hit areas and the carve-out** needed no change either. T296 to T303 had built them, and T170 holds them on the page.
