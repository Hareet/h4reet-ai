## What changed and why

User Story 5: every readable word is drawn live from approved text. The branch has 11 commits on 7cb0235 before this note: 49 files, 3,507 insertions and 156 deletions. Every file is under `apps/web/` or `specs/`, but `.prettierignore`.

- **The faces' core** (`src/room/faces/`, T183):

  - `display-list.ts` has the operations: fills, rectangles, text runs anchored start, middle, or end, paths, and the badges "Draft", "Sample", and "test".
