## What changed

Two commits: RED `2c374cea`, then GREEN `4b9daf6d`.

- `scripts/studio/line.py`: `ROUNDS` goes from 3 to 4. **`ROUNDS` is one number for every asset, so the dinosaur gets a fourth round too.** The module help says four, not three (the old `line.py:9`, `:16`, `:41`). The brief shape's `1 to {ROUNDS}` message follows the constant.
- Step 3: `line.py brief` takes round *n* after a recorded pass of round *n*−1, within `ROUNDS`, with no findings (refused before at the old `line.py:528-529`). It takes it only while that pass re-checks clean, judged again the same way `line.py round` judges it (`passes_again`). So a forged `pass` in the briefs folder opens no round, just as it passes nothing in `line.py round`. The count is unchanged: the higher of the handoff folders (the studio's) and the results. Nothing resets it, and round 5 is refused by the brief's shape.
- One fix that step 3 needed: re-checking a recorded pass used to judge its brief as if it were new, so any later round's brief counted against it. With round 4's brief written after round 3's pass, `line.py round dog` would have exited 2 ("the brief doesn't pass line.py brief: round: 3 …") instead of printing `passed 3`. A re-check (`rechecking=True`, used only by `passes_again`) no longer counts later rounds. `line.py candidate`, with or without `--record`, still refuses a round that a later one follows. `line.py round` now tries passes from the latest down, so it prints the latest pass (`passed 2` after passes in 1 and 2). That's a small change from "the first pass found", and the reviewer should look at it.
- Docs, in the GREEN commit, each quoting his words above: spec 017's `data-model.md` (Brief `round` 1 to 4; Round result), `contracts/studio-line.md` (`line.py brief`, `line.py round`, the decision page after round 4's failure), `plan.md` §7 (a dated **Departure** paragraph, plus the ADR 0053 summary line), and ADR 0053 decision 4 (a dated *Amended 2026-11-14* line; the decision fixed three rounds).
- Left as they are: `spec.md` FR-028 ("the third round") and spec 017's decision 5 quote. The departure says FR-028 now reads as the fourth round. The completed tasks T040 and T042 in `tasks.md` stay as history. Nothing changed under `corpus/`, `assets/**`, `/srv/studio/`, or any human-only path.
