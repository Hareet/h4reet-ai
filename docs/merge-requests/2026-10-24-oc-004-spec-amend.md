### The carve-out's scope, stated plainly

The Author's "a little deeper" answer (the cube back to 4 ft deep) is recorded as given; the deeper-cube option no longer changes the outcome, because no depth over 4 ft gives the pair an exclusive core either (the core is screen-fixed and the wall is the wall).

### The word face's extension, stated plainly

The two recommendations put the sign at 2.6, the smallest scale that meets the desktop floor and fits inside the window (the floor is met first at 2.5429, where the counter faces are exactly 9 px on the desktop). At 2.6 the sign's word face is 6.31 px on the reference phone, under the 9 px floor, while the counters' exemption already stands. Measured, no scale both fits the sign inside the window and passes the word face on the phone: the window's opening holds the sign only to 3.3898 (2.0 m wide), where the word face is 8.26 px; the cube holds it only to 3.616 (2.13 m), where the word face is 8.82 px; and the word face meets the floor at 3.69 (9.00 px), where the sign stands 2.18 m wide, past both. So the taken recommendation is consistent only if the word face shares the phone's exemption, and the rationale that exempts the counters' figures reaches the word: the sign's word appears in the text copy's "Around the room" as the counters' figures do (FR-063, turn-cost.md §8).

It is recorded as Confirmation 25, a yes keeps it and a no reverses it, beside the Author's words and not in their place: it is a measured consequence of "Take both recommendations.", not an Author word, and the four blocks above stand unchanged. The exemption is identified structurally, so the rule and the code name no piece: the readable faces of a piece that carries a region whose `signal` is `turn-cost`. The sign's `call` and `visit` faces carry that signal; its word face's region is a `ui` region, which is why the exemption reaches the word through the piece, not through its region. The sign's second line is marked `atmosphere` and was already out of the floor's scope.
