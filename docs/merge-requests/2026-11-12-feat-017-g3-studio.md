### Departures from the plan, recorded in the same commit
- **A round's result is a file**: `line.py candidate --record` writes `<briefs>/<asset>/<round>.result.json` once (a second `--record` exits 2). `line.py round` needs a record of round 3's failure to print `decide`, and no brief is written after it, so the briefs alone can't say. The next brief must follow a recorded failure and carry its findings verbatim.
- **`line.py round` prints five states**: `1`, `open N`, the next round's number, `passed N`, `decide`. The contract said "the next round, or `decide`".
- **The intended `assets/` path is the record's `directory`**, which the arrival check already requires to be the folder it's in; not under `assets/policy/`.
- **`candidate` takes `--repo`** (default: the working directory) for the repository whose main gives the check and its policy.
- **The ComfyUI test lives in `scripts/studio/`**: `deploy/night-shift/tests/` isn't in the Makefile's `PYTEST_DIRS`, and G3 doesn't edit the Makefile.
