**Why a switch.** A plan block is made before its gate, and the gate before its applies. So W1's three blocks are made at one tree before any of W1's applies. A resource that needs another root's W1 apply waits behind `local.wired = false`, which sits in each root's protected `handover.tf`. Your P-W2 sets it `true`, and W2's blocks are made after W1's applies. What waits:
- the start function, the guard, their permissions, and the hours' schedules, which need digests;
- the rules and the alarm rule that target those functions;
- the model action's second target, a role prod makes;
- the certificate, the distribution, and the records, which need the name servers on the zone;
- the probe's schedule and alarm, which need the site;
- EC2's replacement.

**Why the ID rule, not only the switch.** A switch alone would make T055, T093, and T129 plannable in W2, but it would leave T097 impossible. Any later replace of EC2, or a move of the tunnel end to another zone, as its runbook does, would orphan the account's references. Mending that would take a second account plan after the replace's apply, and no gate holds one. So the resources that name an instance or a health check live in the root that makes it. The functions and their IAM stay in the account, so research.md R-02's reason holds: they don't share a root with the instances they act on. Between roots, a reference is a fixed name only.

## Cost and degraded-mode impact

- **Cost:** EC2 on spot at 12 hours a day is about $8.70 to $13.00 a month for `t4g.large`, against about $17.40 to $26.00 around the clock (research.md R-05, trackers, to confirm). RDS's about $14 goes. The session's sums of R-05's lines put the month at about $38 to $42 with RDS, so about $24 to $28 before the tunnel end's larger type, its data volume, and the backups, which T167 prices against your $45. The schedules and their invocations are inside the free tiers (to confirm).
- **Degraded modes:**
  - outside the hours with the laptop off, every dynamic request gets `no_host_serving`, the room's resting message, and the public view's "no host serving", which SC-001's "EC2 stopped on purpose" already excepts;
  - the shared state, gone or being restored, is degraded everywhere, as RDS's was;
  - while the tunnel end is gone, visitors already get the edge's `no_host_serving`, with or without the store;
  - until W2, a server-budget breach still stops EC2, since the server action is W1's, but EC2 runs only by your hand.

### Question 1a: Which "Mountain Standard Time"? (ADR 0048, decision 1; T116, T117, T124)

| Option | Answer | Implications |
|---|---|---|
| A (recommended) | The local clock with daylight saving, `America/Denver`: 7am to 7pm all year | EventBridge Scheduler takes the name directly. In UTC the window is 14:00 to 02:00 in winter and 13:00 to 01:00 in summer. The functions need the time-zone database, to confirm in their base |
| B | A fixed UTC−7 all year, `Etc/GMT+7` | 14:00 to 02:00 UTC all year, which by the summer clock is 8am to 8pm. No time-zone database needed |

Recommendation: A. It is 7am to 7pm by the clock all year, and Scheduler handles the changes.
