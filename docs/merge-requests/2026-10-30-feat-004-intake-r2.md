### Your own photos: what it would take

How the intake treats a photo used as-is, as P-5 amends it:

- **The records** have no way to say it. A step is `by: model` or `by: hand` (spec 003's data-model.md §2.1). R1 refuses any other `by`, and the scrubber's `--record` exits 2 on one. A photo used as-is could only be recorded as one hand step, and that calls for `digitalCreation`, the wrong term for a photo.
- **The scrubber** strips the camera's EXIF, XMP, and GPS, and writes the one mark the record calls for. It can't write `digitalCapture`, and M5's container rules refuse a term outside the three.
- **The approval can't pass.** A photo source needs your approval (P1), and every approval confirms `recreation_not_photo: true` (spec 003's FR-016; data-model.md §4). An unedited photo can't truthfully confirm that, and P1 refuses an approval whose confirmation isn't true.
- **The spec refuses it.** Spec 003's FR-002: "THE REPOSITORY SHALL hold no source photo, and no unmodified crop of one. IF a file carries camera metadata, THEN THE ARRIVAL CHECK SHALL refuse it. WHEN an asset derives from a photo, THE AUTHOR'S APPROVAL (FR-016) SHALL confirm that it is the studio's recreation, not the photo." And spec 003 quotes the intent's privacy promise: "what's published is the pipeline's recreation, not the photo (intent 002)."

So a capture mark on its own would mark files the intake must refuse. What it would take:

1. **Your words first.** Amend spec 003's FR-002 and FR-016, and the intents' promise, to say the repository may hold your own photos as taken. These are yours to change: an intent or spec amendment, then `/speckit-clarify` and the plan.
2. **The record:** a way to say "the Author's own photo, as taken". That could be a third `by` value, or a field, in spec 003's data model and contracts/asset-record.md. R1, R6, the scrubber's `--record`, and the credits would read it.
3. **The marks:** `digitalCapture` as a fourth term in M5's container rules and C1's table. Also the term a photo with edits, or with a model's step, calls for, which you would read at IPTC's vocabulary.
4. **The approval:** `recreation_not_photo` able to read `"not_applicable"` for such a file, in P1 (a patch, as C5 is) and in spec 003's data model §4. The likeness and the photo source keep your approval by hash, as the main session said.
5. **Where it lives:** not under `assets/twin/`, where M5 holds the model's mark, and not in the room, where spec 004's FR-024 keeps photos in the photos folder and P-7's Room data pass flags one. It would sit in a folder of its own under `assets/`. `REVIEW.md`'s arriving-assets pass, which holds each file to ADR 0010's art direction, would need a clause for it.
