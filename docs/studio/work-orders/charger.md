## Export rules

From `apps/web/src/room/glb/rules.ts`, the rules the fit check applies to the scrubbed file (FR-015 to FR-022).

### The file (FR-015, FR-020)

Binary glTF 2.0, one per piece, named for it: `<piece>.glb`, since a model file's stem is its piece's ID. FR-020: "A STATE SHALL be a separate file or a separate placement, never an animation", so a state is a switch's pose, or a piece of its own with its own file. Textures embedded, as PNG, Huffman-coded RGB JPEG, or still WebP.

### Units and axes (FR-015)

Metres; +Y up; the front facing +Z. In Blender: model the piece facing the front view (−Y) and export with "+Y Up", which turns Blender's −Y into glTF's +Z.

### Size and origin (FR-015, FR-019, FR-051)

The catalog's size times its stylized scale (FR-051), with the origin at the dimension sheet's origin point, so the model's bounds fall on the catalog's expected bounds at that scale.

### Geometry and materials only (FR-015)

No light, camera, animation, shape key, armature or skin, custom property (`extras`), mesh or texture compression (Draco, meshopt, KTX2), or GPU instancing. Extensions only from `KHR_materials_*` (never `KHR_materials_variants`, `KHR_materials_transmission`, or `KHR_materials_volume`), `KHR_texture_transform`, `KHR_mesh_quantization`, and `EXT_texture_webp`.

### Names (FR-018)

Objects and meshes named in lowercase letters, digits, and single hyphens (`desk-top`, `leg-2`): no `.001` suffix, no file name, path, or camera's file stem. The rule: `^[a-z0-9]+(-[a-z0-9]+)*$`.

### Live faces (FR-017, FR-021)

Each its own object, named for its role: one quad in one plain colour with no texture, sized from the dimension sheet, its texture coordinates filling 0 to 1 in reading order. Blender's UV editor puts the face's bottom-left at (0, 0); its exporter flips v, so the GLB stores (0, 0) at the top-left.

### Textures (FR-022, FR-053)

Painted, procedural, or made by an allowed model from a recorded prompt; never projected, traced, or cropped from a photo. sRGB, 8 bits a channel, power-of-two sides, no larger than the piece's budget: 1,024 pixels on the small props the caps name.

### Materials (FR-022)

Flat base colours. No emission, no ambient-occlusion map, and no light, shadow, glow, or outline baked into a texture or built as geometry: the look adds them at run time.

### No words (FR-016)

No word, digit, logo, trademark, key legend, label, regulatory print, tyre-wall text, sticker, screen content, or third-party art. The Author's own non-word sign graphics may stay in a texture; the Author's lettering may not (answer 4), since the room draws it live.

### What the fit check refuses

- **RX1** The container. The file can't be read (in an image build without the room-assets context, every model is unreadable); it doesn't start with glTF and version 2; its declared length differs from the file's; a chunk isn't 4-byte padded within it; or its chunks aren't one JSON chunk followed by at most one BIN chunk
- **RX2** The JSON. It isn't UTF-8 or isn't an object; it holds a duplicate key, NaN, or Infinity; a property outside the glTF 2.0 schema (spec 003's M4 walk, ported); `asset.version` other than "2.0"; an index that names no element; a node with two parents, or in a cycle; more than one buffer, or a buffer whose `byteLength` doesn't match the BIN chunk beyond its padding; an accessor outside its buffer view, or a buffer view outside the BIN chunk
- **RX3** Anything that moves or isn't geometry: a camera (`cameras`, `node.camera`), an animation, a skin (`skins`, `node.skin`), a morph target (`primitive.targets`, `mesh.weights`, `node.weights`), `extras` anywhere (`mesh.extras.targetNames` included), or a `uri` on an image or a buffer
- **RX4** An extension. A name, in `extensionsUsed`, in `extensionsRequired`, or as an extension object, other than `KHR_xmp_json_ld` (where RX5 allows it), `KHR_texture_transform`, `KHR_mesh_quantization`, `EXT_texture_webp`, or a `KHR_materials_*`; any of `KHR_materials_variants`, `KHR_materials_transmission`, and `KHR_materials_volume`; an extension object that `extensionsUsed` doesn't name, or that sits where its specification doesn't put it; `extensionsRequired` naming one `extensionsUsed` doesn't. So a light (`KHR_lights_punctual`), compression (`KHR_draco_mesh_compression`, `EXT_meshopt_compression`, `KHR_texture_basisu`), and GPU instancing (`EXT_mesh_gpu_instancing`) are refused
- **RX5** The mark: missing (the reason reads "not scrubbed"); in any other form than `KHR_xmp_json_ld`'s one packet on the root, holding the digital source type alone, referenced by the asset; with a value outside Amendment C1's (held now: `trainedAlgorithmicMedia` and `compositeWithTrainedAlgorithmicMedia`, IPTC's term for hand-made work joining them once the Author confirms it); or `KHR_xmp_json_ld` anywhere but the root's packet and the asset's reference
- **RX6** A name. A node or mesh `name` that doesn't match `^[a-z0-9]+(-[a-z0-9]+)*$`; any `name` holding a path, a file name, or a camera's file stem (spec 003's M8, ported: a slash or a backslash; a dot followed by 1 to 5 letters, Blender's `.L` and `.R` side suffixes aside; `IMG_`, `PXL_`, `MVIMG_`, `DSC`, `DSCN`, `Screenshot`, WhatsApp's `IMG-YYYYMMDD-WA`, Samsung's `YYYYMMDD_HHMMSS`); a material, image, texture, or sampler `name`, where there is one, other than the scrubber's neutral ID, `<kind>-<its own index>`
- **RX7** The bounds. On any axis, either corner of the model's bounds lies off the catalog's expected corner at its stylized scale by more than the larger of 0.02 m and 3% of the expected extent on that axis at that scale
- **RX8** A live face. For a role the catalog entry lists: no node of that name, or more than one; a node with children, or whose mesh another node also uses, or holding more than one primitive; a primitive other than four vertices forming two triangles; a texture coordinate off the quad's corners by more than 0.001; a texture on its material, or a `COLOR_0`; a centre or an edge more than 1 mm off the catalog's; a facing, an up, a corner's angle, or either triangle's winding more than 1° off
- **RX9** A texture's format. A `mimeType` other than `image/png`, `image/jpeg`, or `image/webp`, or one its bytes don't match; a PNG other than 8 bits a channel in RGB or RGBA; a JPEG other than Huffman-coded (baseline, extended, or progressive), 8-bit, with three components; an animated WebP; a side that isn't a power of two; on a small prop (a piece the caps' `small_props` lists), a side over `small_prop_texture_px`
- **RX10** Baked light in a material: an `emissiveFactor` other than `[0, 0, 0]`, an `emissiveTexture`, `KHR_materials_emissive_strength`, or an `occlusionTexture`
