For the last {{slot:career.ai_strategy_years}} years, Hareet designed Medic's AI strategy with Andra, our CEO, around the two tracks, and led its implementation. He researched where other AI trials and pilots were aiming, mostly clinical decision support and chat support on the CHW side, then turned the tracks into the implementation ideas that mattered most to stakeholders, with supporting the CHW as the top priority. One track puts smarter tools in health workers' hands; the other makes a platform radically easier to build on. He calls them runtime AI and buildtime AI, words he credits to Taylor Downs of OpenFn: the tracks are Hareet's and Andra's; the words are Taylor's. He doesn't have to own the phrasing to be able to use the words, and he can give credit to a source that inspired him.
