The on-call agent squad is agent-watchdog and claude-medic-aws, agents on call for the CHT projects Medic hosts, with an agent orchestrator over both. Hareet configured the orchestrator: a supervisor agent over agent-watchdog and claude-medic-aws, for knowledgeable summaries of all project and infrastructure incidents: on demand, routinely, and ready to serve to Medic's community. Of the two agents under it, agent-watchdog monitors all the CHT projects Medic knows of, and it flags and never acts; claude-medic-aws is a security-first, read-only infrastructure agent.
