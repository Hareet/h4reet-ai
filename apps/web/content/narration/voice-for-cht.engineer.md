Hareet designed Voice for CHT, the idea, the project, and the capability, as the strategy's runtime-AI answer to its top priority, supporting the CHW. The CHW speaks the visit and the form fills itself, offline, and she confirms before anything saves. He found its speech-model partner, helped them fine-tune models to {{slot:voice.model_size}} so they run beside the app on a rural health worker's {{slot:voice.phone_memory}} Android phone, and built Medic's side; the hardware specs illustrate the impact of fine-tuning models for offline low-resource phones. The speech-model partner brings the offline speech models; Medic, the CHT platform and integration. The pilot is underway, and field results come later, published by the brief's own rules.
