Hareet co-owns Denver Local AI Solutions with his wife. In the site's words, every document goes down one of two lanes, and a written rule picks the lane. Local AI keeps sensitive data on hardware the client owns or leases, in their office or their own data center. Before anything can go to cloud AI, cleaning software swaps names, account numbers, and other identifiers for tags, and every cloud request shows the cleaned text that went out, so staff and owners can check it. The site is live now, at denverlocal.ai.
