## The circuit

The three adapters sit in a column on the right, reached by an out-bus and a return bus.

Beads (functions of time through `OM.clock`): white questions, green local answers, gold paid answers, crimson red-team cases, grey withheld lines. A gate goes green while an answer passes it; `amounts` goes crimson for the withheld answer; `ownership` turns dashed grey for "can't run". The circuit comes back three times, each a hard cut on a word: the guards (with the breath), the live runs, and a topic closing.

The live red-team runs go through `POST /admin/twin/turn` on one adapter (`redteam/run.py:35-39`), the turn route, so their answers meet the guards; the film shows them bypassing the room and the edge.
