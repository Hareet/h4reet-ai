## Steps

Copy them to `tools/voice-kokoro-raw.wav` and `tools/voice-kokoro-raw.words.json` (only if those don't exist yet). Edit `tools/respace.py`'s `PLAN`: one entry per paragraph of `script.txt`, `(first word, nth-ignored, new start in s)`, in order (the matcher takes the next occurrence of the word after the previous paragraph). Put a 0.5 s lead, ~0.8–1.3 s between paragraphs, and a 1.5–3 s word-free gap where the circuit needs a burst to breathe; keep the film ≤ 95 s. Run `python3 -I tools/respace.py` from the film folder: it writes `assets/voice.wav` (normalised to −16 LUFS), `assets/voice.words.json`, and `captions.srt` (it asserts the paragraph count matches `script.txt`).
2. **Embed the cues.** In `index.html`, replace the JSON inside `<script type="application/json" id="om-cues">…</script>` with the respaced words (keep the other keys; set `duration`), and set `data-duration` on `<audio id="om-voice">` to the new duration. (b2's scaffold copy is there now; your film replaces its body and script.) Remove any `<audio id="om-sound">` tag copied from b2 (the Author's `om sound` writes a fresh one).
3. **Write the word-time table** `W` from the respaced `assets/voice.words.json` (as b2 does) and build the film on it.
4. **Check it runs.** Chrome can't start in this sandbox, so you can't render or screenshot. Run the jsdom probe:
   the session's jsdom probe (not committed): `node probe.cjs <film dir> 0,10,30,60,80`
   It must print `errors: none` and the duration. Also `node --check` the extracted script. Check by reading your own CSS/positions that nothing overlaps or runs off the 1920×1080 frame at the zoom it's seen at (b2's lessons: alerts go bottom-right; a camera push must not crop labels; the first frame must already show the subject; no still stretch over ~2 s outside a planned stop or end hold).
5. **Write `treatment.md`** (shot list with cut words, the circuit's nodes and bead flows, what each shot's source is) and **`materials.md`**.
6. **Don't commit, don't touch other films' folders, don't run `make`, don't edit anything outside your film's folder.** Report back: the film's duration, its shot list (one line each, with the cut word and time), every on-screen number with its source, anything you left out because you couldn't source it, and the probe output.

## Lessons from the first renders (apply from the start)

- **Fill the frame.** Kinetic shots came out too small in the first renders (content in the top-left third, mono ~15 px on screen). Standard: slammed words 110–400 px, mono ≥ 40 px, content spanning ~80% of the width and centred vertically; source captions ≥ 22 px pinned at the bottom. Fewer lines, bigger.
- **Never still.** Every kinetic shot pushes (scale 1 → 1.08–1.15 over its hold, accelerating), new lines land with a slam, and a cut changes the frame decisively (scale, position, or ground). Circuit holds zoom-drift and keep beads flowing. No still stretch over ~2 s outside a planned stop or the end hold.
- **Word timings by lookup.** Read `W` from the embedded cues by word and occurrence (`films/local-first/index.html` does this), so a re-voice only needs a new respace `PLAN`. Check the raw take's word list against `script.txt`: Kokoro sometimes splits words ("ne / two / rk"); merge any splits before respacing.
- **Long films.** The next two run about 2½ minutes: keep each ≤ 175 s, with 0.6–1.2 s between paragraphs and one or two 1.5–2.5 s breaths where the film turns.
