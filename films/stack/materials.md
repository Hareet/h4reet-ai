## Drawn, not data

- The "Dropped at the queue" panel's line and the daily summary's grey rows are drawn shapes with no axis, value or row text: they show a panel rising and a summary with no values yet. No number is drawn on them.
- Bead rates (a request every 0.62 s, a Grafana read every 1.3 s) are pacing, not measured traffic; the probe interval is the real `interval_ms`.
- The waiting bars in valkey and seaweedfs and the collector's queue bar have no numbers.
