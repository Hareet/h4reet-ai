## The circuit

One world (2640 × 1420), front-on, flat, moved by `OM.camera`; beads are functions of time (`OM.clock`).

- Nodes: THE ROOM (with a small neon sign whose "this call" reads the last reply home), EDGE, GATEWAY ("prices each call"), MODEL-SERVER ("llama.cpp, on the GPU", $0), ANTHROPIC API (outside the host's dashed wall; Claude Haiku, the paid fallback), GATEWAY-STATE (the meter, in micro-dollars: a six-decimal reading and the integer µUSD), OTEL COLLECTOR → LANGFUSE and → GRAFANA (inside the wall; dim until the narration names them).
- Beads: a question (white) runs room → edge → gateway; a local call goes to the model server and back (green); a paid call goes out through the wall to the API and back (gold); the answer (pale green) runs back to the room, where the sign's "this call" changes to "local · $0" or "$0.001125 est."; a paid call's cost bead (small gold) drops into GATEWAY-STATE, which counts up by that call's micro-dollars. Once Langfuse lights, every call also sends a thin span bead gateway → collector → Langfuse; once Grafana lights, another to Grafana. In "decides", each paid call first touches the meter (the admission reservation) before it leaves. In "run", 186 calls start at the gateway (the eval runner's admin endpoint, `evals/run.py:6-7`, "charged to prod's meter") and the meter counts that run's own per-case costs, in the file's order, to $0.209331.
- It comes back three times, each entered on a hard cut on a word, in its state at that moment: the call (¶3, three framings), the meter and Langfuse (¶6–7, three framings), the decision and the run (¶10–11, two framings).
