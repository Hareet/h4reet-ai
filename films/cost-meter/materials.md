## The per-call figure (derived)

The brief asks for one Haiku turn's cost, derived from the 186-case run and labelled as that run's average:

- `evals/product/history/2026-11-10T063144Z-anthropic.json:9` `"cases": 186`; `:16` `"input_tokens": 1977052`; `:17` `"output_tokens": 23091`; `:14` `"cost_micro_usd": 209331`.
- `config/prices.yaml:65-74` (version 2026-11-09.1, "Anthropic's pricing page, Claude Haiku 5.5: $0.10 input and $0.50 output per million tokens, read 2026-11-09"): `anthropic` / `claude-haiku-5-5`, input 0.1, output 0.5.
- (1,977,052 × 0.10 + 23,091 × 0.50) ÷ 1,000,000 = $0.2092507 for the run; ÷ 186 = **$0.0011250 a turn, 1,125 µUSD** (1,125.004). The recorded total ÷ 186 gives 1,125.4 µUSD, the same $0.001125 at six decimals. Tokens a turn: 1,977,052 ÷ 186 = 10,629.3 → **10629**; 23,091 ÷ 186 = 124.1 → **124**.
- On screen it is labelled "the 186-case run's average" (price shot); the sign, the reply body, the span card and the stop show it without the label. The circuit's meter adds 1,125 µUSD for each paid call it shows (k × 1,125: $0.001125 … $0.016875 by the decision shot). These are illustrations of that average, not recorded calls.
