## Departures from the sources, on screen

- No emoji glyphs; the struck-through items are drawn with a bar.
- The guards light in the narration's order (numbers, amounts, ownership, privacy); the gateway runs them in its own order (`services/gateway/src/twin/guards.ts:6-17`), whole-answer guards first and the sentence checks after.
- The narration is Kokoro's `af_heart` reading `script.txt`, respaced paragraph by paragraph (`tools/respace.py`; the raw take is `tools/voice-kokoro-raw.wav` and `.words.json`) and normalised to −16 LUFS. Captions: `captions.srt`, from the script's words at the respaced times.
