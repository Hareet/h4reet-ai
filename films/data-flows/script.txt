Every byte that leaves this stack is written down before it can leave.

The data-flow disclosure lists each flow: where it goes, what it carries, what it never carries, and how to switch it off.

And the build enforces it: a service without a row is refused.

When you talk to the twin, what leaves your device? Your typed words, the last few turns, and the address and browser every request carries.

Never audio, the avatar, a cookie, or any identifier of the visit or the visitor. And in production, no one's words land in any log, trace, or store.

When the paid fallback answers, your words go to Anthropic. In Hareet's words: this site saves none of it, and Anthropic may keep a turn it answered for up to thirty days, never for training.

Then, what the twin says back. It answers only from what Hareet wrote, and when that doesn't hold the answer, it says: "I don't know yet."

Before any word leaves the gateway, code checks the answer against its sources: numbers, amounts, ownership, privacy. A failed check withholds the answer.

And every change is tested: a hundred and eighty-six cases drawn from the corpus, graded by code, never by another model.

Every data flow disclosed. Built in the open, at hareet dot A I.
