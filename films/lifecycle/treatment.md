## The circuit: the pipeline

The spine is the lifecycle as a circuit, left to right in two rows, with the wire from TASKS returning to IMPLEMENT the way a line of text returns:

```
INTENT → SPEC → PLAN → TASKS
   ┌─────────────────────┘
IMPLEMENT → REVIEW → GATE → MERGE (Hareet)
```

- Each stage is a node that lights green as the narration reaches it and leaves its artifact behind as a chip: `intent/NNN-*.md`, `spec.md`, `plan.md`, `tasks.md`, `feat/NNN-*`, the merge-request note. The command lines in the nodes are `CLAUDE.md:7`'s.
- One change travels it as a white bead, one stage per shot. In the intent shot, more sentences arrive from off-frame (whoever wants something).
- The agents stand beside their stages as role cards with their one-line limits: spec-writer above SPEC; implementer, reviewer and red-teamer below IMPLEMENT, REVIEW and GATE (the gate runs the red-team suite, `tools/gates/gate:193`); ops below RELEASE; docs in the incident shot.
- MERGE is drawn as a person's step: a paper-white node with a person drawn in crimson, and below it the one card that isn't an agent, "Hareet · only a human merges to main".
- Production sits off to the right and appears only on its shots: a GATEWAY that trips and drafts its own incident (the draft bead runs back along a top wire to INTENT: the next intent), and the deploy: RELEASE → EC2 and LAPTOP, labelled "spec 001 · approved 2026-11-10 · as specced".

The camera is front-on and follows what lands (v4, after the first render's notes). Each circuit shot is a list of subjects in world coordinates (the active node with its artifact, agent card or tags): the cut frames the first subject at about 78% of the frame (zoom capped at ×2.2), each later subject is reached by a 0.7 s move, and every hold drifts in 6–12%. Lit wires carry a stream of small beads in every circuit hold, so the circuit is never still. Neighbouring nodes may be cropped at the frame edge; the subject never is.

## Layout and motion checks (read from the CSS, the camera targets and a jsdom pass)

- Circuit framings: each subject box is fitted at 78% of the frame and drifts at most 12% further, so it stays inside; the boxes were checked so no subject label is cut (the trip shot's last box takes in TASKS whole; the deploy shot's both-hosts frame is a fixed pose with its left edge in the GATE/MERGE gap).
- Sizes on screen: node titles 58 px world, 54–128 px at the zooms used (×0.94–2.2), 43 px in the two wide shots (×0.74–0.79); node and card text 32 px world, 30–70 px on screen except the wide shots.
- Kinetic shots are centred vertically (content between about y 170 and 910) and push linearly from their first frame, ×1.12 (×1.16 for the 10.9 s five-things shot); landed lines drift 30–60 px until the next cut; the widest lines stay inside the frame at the end of the push.
- The five-things shot opens on its header at ×1.7, centred, which moves into place as "telemetry" lands; the clarify shot types the question as it's said.
- A jsdom pass at 0.1 s steps finds no window of 1 s or more where a kinetic shot scales under 1.2%/s or a circuit hold has neither that zoom rate nor a moving bead.
