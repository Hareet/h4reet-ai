# Intent: Dog modeled and brought in
Author: the Author (his order of 2026-11-13, item 3 d), drafted by the night-shift spec-writer. Status: draft. Created: 2026-11-13.

## Problem
The room's fourth avatar, the dog, is "a generic dog", a flat cut-out (intent 000: "to be replaced with final characters later"). Spec 009 built the path for a model, its clips, and its fallback, but the dog has no model, so a visitor who chooses it sees the flat shape in 3D too.

## Proposed outcome
His item, word for word:

> 3. d) "dog modeled and brought in". His dog's photos: `/srv/studio/briefs/dog/photos/`. His consent: "it's okay that we use the likeness of my dog".

The dog avatar shows a modeled dog in the 3D room, made from those photos, where it now shows the cut-out. Until he approves the model's look, the cut-out stays.

His order for the night, as the brief gives it: "Okay, we want the night-shift, in order of importance, from the"improvements, in your words" section: a) "better HUD with examples provided, and load screen", c) "avatar walking leg animations", d) "dog modeled and brought in", e) "dinosaur modeled and brought in", g) "hareet-ai corpus from film scripts", "b) "re-do PDF resume with links from films", i) "dino being able to eat chunks of assets like desk, busy sign, laptop, chalkboard", h) "twin and dog running from dino" .... I will give you the examples of the HUD and placement of the interface layout, and provide you photos of my dog to model and it's okay that we use the likeness of my dog."

## Affected users and systems
Visitors who choose the dog; the Author (his look approval of the asset); the studio (a later round that models it); the room's build and 3D part (spec 009's avatars); lookdev.

## Constraints
The studio's tools list on main (P-7) only. Every file a photo touches follows the studio's photo rules. No file is published before his look approval. The constitution's Principle I (no biometric data from visitors) and VIII (the AI is disclosed) hold.

## Expected cost impact
None in the cloud. Dev-time model spend for the spec, the code, and the review; the studio's work runs on his GPU.

## Data-flow impact
None expected: the model is a static file from the site's own origin, as the other avatars are.

## Open questions
