## Two lanes

- `[A-DOC site]` Every document goes down one of two lanes, and a written rule picks the lane. Local AI keeps sensitive data on hardware the client owns or leases, in their office or their own data center. Cloud AI improves fastest and is good for research, drafting, and ideas.
- `[A-DOC site]` Before anything can go to cloud AI, cleaning software swaps names, account numbers, and other identifiers for tags. Every cloud request shows the cleaned text that went out, so staff and owners can check it.
- `[A 2026-11-06]` What happens to anything bound for cloud AI, in his words: "Anything bound for Cloud AI is pre and post processed by custom software we create alongside your team that ensures no sensitive material ever touches Cloud AI".
- `[A 2026-11-06]` Asked whether to keep that guarantee, a promise a client could hold him to, in his words: "Keep that guarantee, it's okay. We are professionals and will try to meet that standard, and customers should be aware of the risks that any professional inherently takes."
- `[A-DOC site]` The client's team writes the rules with Denver Local AI. Anything from a tax return takes the local lane even after cleaning, and so does a law office's matter file unless the client consents.
- `[A-DOC site]` What the client owns: the software and the written rules ("They're yours, not a subscription."); a test report (identifiers planted in sample files, how many the cleaning caught, and every miss listed); a "What leaves your office" sheet for their security plan; and the freedom to switch AI companies, because the software works with any model.

## How they work `[A-DOC site]`

1. `[A-DOC site]` Learn the tasks: which chores eat staff time, which files they touch, and how the team uses AI today, including personal ChatGPT accounts.
2. `[A-DOC site]` Clean up the files: de-duplicate folders and records, then build the cleaning software, tested on the client's own documents with every miss listed.
3. `[A-DOC site]` Set up private AI on the client's own hardware: a local model on a computer in the office, a server behind their VPN, or their own data center, with no per-use fees. Depending on the hardware, it can run with the internet switched off.
4. `[A-DOC site]` Build the pathways with the team: for each task, a written rule and the software that follows it (local or cloud AI, which tool, and what gets cleaned first), and an AI section for their security plan.
5. `[A-DOC site]` Train the team and stay close: staff practise with their own questions, then a weekly visit and regular catch-ups, with the rules updated when the tools change.
