## Readings for your eye

Each is the session's reading, beside your words, which stay as they are.

1. **The guarantee and the site.** Golden Q&A 2 says your software "ensures no sensitive material ever touches Cloud AI". The site's lines in the same section promise "a test report (identifiers planted in sample files, how many the cleaning caught, and every miss listed)". Both stay. A visitor who asks whether a miss is possible meets both, and your reason now sits beside them in `07`, so the twin can give it: "We are professionals and will try to meet that standard, and customers should be aware of the risks that any professional inherently takes."
2. **Two promises about the twin.** `08`'s Golden Q&A 5 says "your conversation is not saved" and "Langfuse keeps only costs and usage, and we use Masking." The twin isn't built yet, so these bind its spec. The project already keeps payloads out of telemetry unless `TELEMETRY_PAYLOAD_SAMPLING` is on (`CLAUDE.md`), so turning that sampling on would break "your conversation is not saved". `docs/data-flow-disclosure.md` will have to say the same before the topic opens.
3. **The repository link.** Until the repository is public, Golden Q&A 2's link won't open for visitors, as the interviewer's note says.
4. **The laptop.** As the interview recorded it, only the first half of Golden Q&A 3's laptop sentences matched the room. "If the laptop is plugged in, that means we are using local models" is spec 004's FR-042: the charger shows plugged in only while the tier is local. "Otherwise we have failed over to a cloud solution" doesn't match:
   - FR-043 gives unknown, paused, and not-live a pose of their own, "distinct from the plugged and the cloud poses", the plug resting on the desk, and the degraded classes (resting, budget, unavailable) share it.

   - The twin never receives a live value (FR-038), so it can't say which pose a visitor sees.

   Asked in the session, you approved the session's wording, so Golden Q&A 3 now says: "Otherwise we have failed over to a cloud solution, or the room doesn't know the live state yet, and the kill switch to ensure we are cost monitoring and effective." Your note B also puts the laptop on your own desk, which the room shows. It names no place, and spec 002's FR-050 location cases judge it before the topic opens.
5. **Your experience at Medic.** Golden Q&A 8's "creating custom software for MoHs" and "I've done the local and online AI tooling and custom software setups and training on national MoH scales" stay as you approved them. `07` now sets `00`'s ownership line beside your answer, verbatim: "Medic: part of a human-centered software team building CHT for CHWs; Kenya's Ministry of Health owned the workflows and product decisions. Local AI deployed "internally to their datacenters" is new to the corpus, and no ruling in `01` covers it. It's your account, `[A]`, which the twin may give as yours.

Today the PDF carries no contact: `main`'s `assets/policy/resume-header.json` has `"full_name": null` and `"contact": null`, and spec 002's FR-017 gives the PDF the interactive resume's header while either is empty.
