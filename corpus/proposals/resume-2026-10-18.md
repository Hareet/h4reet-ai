## What this is

The first draft of the room's resume source, `apps/web/content/resume.yaml` (contracts/resume-source.md §1). It is not a corpus change: the resume is a rendering of the corpus, and every item cites the corpus sentences it rests on. The build checks each citation against the corpus (C1–C9, §2), and `apps/web/content/resume.lock.json` pins the five corpus files the draft reads, so a change to one of them fails `make test` until the lock is rewritten and you've read what changed.

The draft stays a draft until you approve it. Until then, a build without drafts publishes only "Draft, not approved", in the room and in the PDF, and dev's drafts build shows it under that banner.

## What you do

1. Read the items below: the draft's words, each with the corpus words it cites. The draft's words are an agent's choice of wording, for you to keep or rewrite in `resume.yaml`; the quotes are the corpus's own.
2. When you're satisfied, in your terminal: `sha256sum apps/web/content/resume.yaml`, then an entry in `assets/policy/content-approvals.json` (`kind: resume`), committed on `main` (contracts/resume-source.md §5). Any later edit to the file makes it a draft again.
3. Optionally, fill `full_name` and `contact` in `assets/policy/resume-header.json` on `main`. With both filled, the PDF's header carries them; with either empty, the PDF carries the room's header, "Hareet" and the two lines below (FR-017). The header isn't proposed here: it's your file, outside the corpus, which keeps the surname out.
4. Run `make corpus-check`, which applies your private denylist to this proposal, whose text repeats every item's words.
