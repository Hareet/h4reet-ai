## 3. The checker's rules (the small test the work stands on)

`scripts/corpus/film_claims.py <proposal>`, exit 0 clean, 1 with one line per problem:

- **R1** each table row names a script (one of the seven sections), a line number, and a repository source; the script line is found verbatim (whitespace-normalised) in its script: for §1 to §6 in `films/SCRIPTS.md`, and where that film's `script.txt` has the sentence, there too; for the seventh, in `films/kill-switch-b2/script.txt`, its lines numbered by sentence, and its source one that `films/kill-switch-b2/materials.md` lists. The split: a sentence ends at `.`, `?`, `!` or `...` followed by whitespace and then a capital letter, an opening quotation mark, or the end of the file; so "At five dollars..." (file line 9) and "it stops itself." (file line 11) are one sentence, since the next word is lower-case; and a quotation is never split, so the quoted message on the file's line 15 is one line. Each row also gives the sentence's file lines, so the Author finds it in `script.txt`.
- **R2** every line in scope appears in the table once, as carried, carried in part, or left out, with a reason from the closed list (FR-006: unbuilt, retracted, a recorded run's result, a currency amount, a moving count, a statement about the Author that no corpus file holds, a duplicate of `08`, contradicted by the repository): `SCRIPTS.md` §1 to §6's numbered lines and every sentence of `films/kill-switch-b2/script.txt`; SC-003's count. A row carried in part names both spans, each found verbatim in the script line, the carried one with the corpus line it became and the left-out one with its reason; for example `kill-switch-b2` file line 5 ("to the millionth of a dollar": currency), file line 7 ("At three fifty": currency), file lines 9 and 11 ("At five dollars": currency), and `SCRIPTS.md` §1 line 2 ("to the millionth of a dollar": currency). A row left out as contradicted by the repository names the file that contradicts it.
- **R3** no carried line is one `SCRIPTS.md` marks "Not in the repository yet", "Not built on main yet", or retracted ("didn't hold up"), by the marker strings; and no carried `kill-switch-b2` sentence cites a `materials.md` row whose Notes say "shown as built" (today, the tunnel's row: "no inbound ports, an outbound tunnel only"). The checker finds the rows by that string; which sentence a row stands under is the table's, and T007's reviewer checks it by hand (the sentence "From outside, there's no door: no inbound ports, only an outbound tunnel, and only the gateway has a road out." cites two rows, the tunnel's and the networks'; it is left out whole, the tunnel clause as unbuilt and the road-out clause as contradicted by the repository, Q115).
- **R4** numbers and money, in the proposal's new prose (the `08` text, the Golden Q&A, the gap and "Do not claim" lines; not the reading guide or the table, which cite the scripts):
  - no number outside a `{{slot:…}}`: a digit run, or a number word from `guards.ts`'s G1 (its `UNITS`, `TENS`, `SCALES`, and the plurals G1 reads, which are its `SCALES`' only: "hundreds", "thousands", "millions", "billions", "dozens"; "one" isn't one);

Its parity tests read `guards.ts` as text: the keys of `UNITS`, `TENS`, and `SCALES` (which also give R4's number words), and each fixed word found in `EXTRA_PATTERN`'s definition.

  - It imports nothing from `corpus/tools`, `redteam`, or the gateway.
