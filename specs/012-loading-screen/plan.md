## Summary

- **The loader starts at "Enter"** (§4): in 3D only, over the room's page, in place of "Loading the room…", and gives way at the first of: every needed file ended, the room in 2D, or a 7-second ceiling on the room's clock. The room's timer, not the view, decides, so a broken view can't hold the hand-off.
- **A pure progress model** (§2): four parts. The 3D code, the room's text, and the typefaces are steps of 10, 2, and 3 percent; the figures' files and the set models share 85 percent by their bytes as served, each counting once its load has ended. The model reads the asset events the room already records, and nothing else.
- **The room's models are shown while the loader shows** (§3): the set models take the gate the figures' files already take, parsing before the hand-off starts or after it ends, never during it; a swap that would land in the hand-off waits for its end. The fetch, its integrity check, and its order don't change.
- **The page behind, tinted by layer** (§5): `data-loading="on"` on the page's root lays a tint over the stage's view, the bar, the panels, and "Around the room", and over nothing else, so the AI disclosure and the stand-in line stay undimmed by construction. The card is spec 005's dark glass with a wide yellow bar.
- **Hints as data** (§7): `apps/web/room/hints.yaml`, checked by spec 004's words family with four new rules, its figures filled at build from the configuration that enforces them, carried filled in the index. A small state machine rotates them every 6 seconds, as the WAI-ARIA carousel pattern pauses and stops.
- **The event, the panel, and the row** (§8): `loading_cut_short`, in the same steps spec 005's `fullscreen_entered` took.

**What the Author does** (§12): approves (T000); gates and merges the branch; looks at the loader on the live room (SC-010); answers FR-035.

## 2. The progress model (`src/room/loading/progress.ts`; contract §1, §2)

### 2.1 What it holds

A frozen table of the four parts and their shares (PD-3), the expected files once given, the set of ended files, and the highest value reached. Each method returns at once and never throws.

### 2.2 Where each part's end comes from

| Part | `app.ts` calls | When |
|---|---|---|
| `code` | `progress.ended("code")` | at "Enter" if the scene module has resolved, else when it does inside `sceneOrLate()` |
| `text` | `progress.ended("text")` | on `loadText(1)`'s first settle, either way |
| `typefaces` | `progress.ended("typefaces")` | on `typefaces.settled()` (PD-15) |
| `files` | `progress.expected(list)`, then `progress.asset(event.asset, event.outcome)` | `stage.expected` resolving; each asset event through `recordAsset`, before its queue |

### 2.3 The stage line and the value text

`view.ts` words the state with the index's UI words: `loading.code`, `loading.pieces` with `{done}` and `{total}`, or `loading.finishing`; and `aria-valuetext` is the value, a percent sign, a colon, and that line ("64%: Loading the room's pieces: 12 of 21"). The percent sign and the colon are the view's, not a UI word's, so RT3's digit rule never meets a number.

## 3. The 3D stage's side (`stage.ts`, `scene.ts`, `scene/loader.ts`; contract §3)

### 3.1 `expected`

`Stage3D` gains `readonly expected: Promise<readonly ExpectedFile[]>`. In `scene.ts`, it resolves inside the existing `Promise.all([import("./scene/loader"), …]).then(…)`, with `modelEntries(part)` and, where the avatars' chunk loaded, `figureEntries(part, options.avatar)`, mapped to `{ asset, bytes }`; with `[]` at once where `load_order` is empty and no avatar loads (the branch that skips the import); and with `[]` in the `catch`. Below the floor, it resolves with the list, and every file then ends `skipped` at once, as the loader records today.

### 3.2 The gates

- `LoaderOptions.modelGate`: in `one()`, a set model awaits `options.modelGate?.() ?? options.handoff` where it awaits `options.handoff` today. `scene.ts` gives the same function as `figureGate`.
- `LoaderOptions.swapGate`: in `parseAndSwap`, after `parseModel` and before `options.swap`, `await options.swapGate?.()`, raced with the loader's `stop()`, so a room that goes to 2D during the wait never swaps and lets its hold go (R-6).
- The lookdev page gives neither, so it loads as today.

### 3.3 What doesn't change

The fetch and its `integrity`, the order, the slots, the holds, the ledger, the events, `stop()`, and `reload()` (contract §3, "Unchanged").

## 4. The room's sequence and the ceiling (`app.ts`, `quality.ts`; contract §4)

### 4.1 The sequence

As contract §4 gives it, in `arrive()`. The changes from today:
- after `renderRoom` and before `layout.heading.focus()`: where `webgl` is true and the scene promise hasn't settled to null, create the model and the view, set loading on, focus the loader's heading, say `loading.heading`, and start the timer;
- `recordAsset` also feeds the model;
- the hand-off starts on `Promise.all([gaveWay, figuresWait() ?? Promise.resolve()])` in place of `figuresWait()` alone;
- `toFlat`, from any road, calls `giveWay("2d")` first when the loader shows;
- `giveWay(why)` runs once: it clears the timer, says the end line, sets loading off, starts the fade, focuses the room's heading, logs, and for `ceiling` with the model not complete emits `loading_cut_short` once a visit.

Where `webgl` is false, nothing above runs, and today's path is unchanged (FR-003).

### 4.2 The ceiling

`LOADING_CEILING_MS = 7000` (PD-7). The timer is the room's clock's `setTimeout`, set at "Enter". `test/room/quality.test.ts` gains the bound: at most 7,000, and at least `SCENE_WAIT_MS` plus the scene's `wait_ms` default (1,500), so the ceiling never cuts today's own waits short.

### 4.3 What if the view or the model throws

The view is built inside a `try`; on a throw, the room logs nothing new (a release build keeps no log), keeps the timer and a bare model, and gives way as above, with no card on screen. A throw inside a later update is caught at the call, and leaves the view as it was. The hand-off is never awaited on anything the view owns (FR-007).

## 5. The view and the page behind (`loading/view.ts`, `loading.css`, `layout.ts`; contract §5)

### 5.1 The tints and inert

PD-9 and PD-18. `test/ui/loading-page.test.ts` reads `loading.css` and checks that every rule under `html[data-loading="on"]` names one of the four tinted selectors and none names `.disclosure`, `.room-notes`, `.room-note`, or `.room-stand-in`.

### 5.2 The DOM

As contract §5 draws it, built with `dom.ts`'s `h`, the words and hints set as text nodes, never as markup. The bar's fraction is set with `style.setProperty("--loader-fraction", String(value / 100))` on the bar's element (FR-052).

### 5.3 Fallbacks

Under `prefers-reduced-transparency: reduce`, `prefers-contrast: more`, and `@supports not (backdrop-filter …)`, the card takes `--glass-alpha: 1` and no blur, as spec 005's fallbacks already set for `.hud-glass` (the card carries the class). Under `forced-colors: active`, the card, the track, and the fill take `Canvas`, `CanvasText`, and `Highlight` (FR-008). Under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, no transition, sheen, or fade (FR-040).

### 5.4 Contrast (FR-041)

`test/room/loading-contrast.test.ts` reuses `test/room/hud-contrast.test.ts`'s compositing: the card's glass over white and over black; white text on it at 4.5:1 or more; the fill (`#ffd23f`) against the track composited over the glass at 3:1 or more; the track's border against the glass at 3:1 or more; the focus ring at 3:1 or more.

## 6. Accessibility (FR-037 to FR-043)

### 6.1 The progress bar

`role="progressbar"`, named by the heading through `aria-labelledby`, with `aria-valuemin`, `aria-valuemax`, `aria-valuenow`, and `aria-valuetext` set at each change. As the ARIA documentation reads, screen readers read a progress bar's value when it is reached or focused, and some announce changes of their own; the room's own announcements are the start and the end (FR-038), so nothing is said for each change. T001 records what NVDA, JAWS, and VoiceOver do.

### 6.2 Focus and the keyboard

At "Enter", focus goes to `#loader-heading` (`tabindex="-1"`). The hint controls are the only Tab stops: the room's parts are inert through `setInteractive(false)` and "Around the room" through `setLoading(true)`. At give-way, focus goes to the room's heading, wherever it was. Nothing traps: Tab leaves the card for the browser's own controls.

### 6.3 The announcements

The room's polite status region (`a11y.ts`'s announcer) says `loading.heading` at the start and `loading.ready` or `loading.cut_short` at the end. The hint's own `aria-live="polite"` `aria-atomic="true"` paragraph says each new hint. The hand-off's own line follows, as today.
