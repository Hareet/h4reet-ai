## Summary

- **The loader starts at "Enter"** (§4): in 3D only, over the room's page, in place of "Loading the room…", and gives way at the first of: every needed file ended, the room in 2D, or a 7-second ceiling on the room's clock. The room's timer, not the view, decides, so a broken view can't hold the hand-off.
- **A pure progress model** (§2): four parts. The 3D code, the room's text, and the typefaces are steps of 10, 2, and 3 percent; the figures' files and the set models share 85 percent by their bytes as served, each counting once its load has ended. It keeps every end it hears, those before the 3D stage's list of files among them, and reads nothing but the asset events the room already records.
- **The room's models are shown while the loader shows** (§3): the set models take the gate the figures' files already take, closed from the loader's giving way to the hand-off's end; a parse begun before it finishes, and swaps when the hand-off ends. The fetch, its integrity check, and its order don't change.
- **The page behind, tinted by element, in each layout** (§5): `data-loading="on"` tints the stage's view, the bar, the panels, and "Around the room", each positioned so its tint stays inside it, and never the AI disclosure or the notes. The card carries its own dark glass in every layout: fixed below the measured strip and the notes' reserve in the column and dock layouts, first in the page's flow in the stacked one. The scene's own motion is held still.
- **Hints as data** (§7): `apps/web/room/hints.yaml`, checked by spec 004's words family with four new rules, each rule's lenient and strict behaviour given; its figures filled at build from the configuration that enforces them, in the image as in a checkout; carried filled in the index. A small state machine rotates them every 6 seconds, as the WAI-ARIA carousel pattern pauses, stops, and restarts.
- **The event, the panel, and the row** (§8): `loading_cut_short`, in the same steps spec 005's `fullscreen_entered` took.

**What the Author does** (§12): accepts or closes the intent and approves (T000); gates and merges the branch; runs his checklist and looks at the loader on the live room (T039); answers FR-035.

## 4. The room's sequence and the ceiling (`app.ts`, `quality.ts`, `motion.ts`; contract §4)

### 4.1 The sequence

As contract §4 gives it, in `arrive()`. The changes from today:
- after `renderRoom` and in place of `layout.heading.focus()`: where `webgl` is true and the scene promise hasn't settled to null, create the model, set loading on, take the motion hold, build the view, focus the loader's heading, create `gaveWay`, and start the timer;
- `recordAsset` also feeds the model, from "Enter";
- `mount3d` hands the stage `gaveWay`;
- the hand-off starts on `Promise.all([gaveWayPromise, figuresWait() ?? Promise.resolve()])` in place of `figuresWait()` alone;
- `toFlat`, from any road, calls `giveWay("2d")` first when the loader shows;
- `giveWay(why)` runs once: it makes the card inert, resolves `gaveWay`, clears the timer, releases the hold, says the end line, starts the fade, sets loading off, focuses the room's heading, logs, and for `ceiling` with the model not complete emits `loading_cut_short` once a visit.

Where `webgl` is false, nothing above runs, and today's path is unchanged (FR-003).

### 4.2 The ceiling

`LOADING_CEILING_MS = 7000` (PD-7). The timer is the room's clock's `setTimeout`, set at "Enter". `test/room/quality.test.ts` gains the bound: at most 7,000, and at least `SCENE_WAIT_MS` plus the scene's `wait_ms` default (1,500), so the ceiling never cuts today's own waits short.

### 4.3 What if the view or the model throws

The view is built inside a `try`; on a throw, the room logs nothing new (a release build keeps no log), keeps the timer and a bare model, and gives way as above, with no card on screen. A throw inside a later update is caught at the call, and leaves the view as it was. The hand-off is never awaited on anything the view owns (FR-007).
