## 2. Technical Context

TypeScript 5 (strict), Vite, vitest with jsdom and axe; `yaml` (already a dependency of the build, as `build/content.ts` reads `messages.yaml`); Python 3 standard library for `line.py` (PNG decoding by `zlib`, as the arrival check does). No new dependency, no browser download, no font. No cloud, no gateway change, no model call at run time. Performance: one fewer compositing pass per panel at quality levels 0 and 1 (no blur); the skin's stylesheet is static; the halves' boxes are computed in the re-fit that computes the others, never per frame.

## 10. Risks and their answers

- **The cascade fight** (B's rules against room.css's and split.css's): B's rules name the root's attributes and the cascade test pins each winner; T-INT removes the dead declarations behind them.
- **The chamfer, the focus ring, the glow, and the hit area** (rework, S-4; rework 2, Q72): a `clip-path` on the control would clip its box's corners (the hit area) and anything drawn outside it, which jsdom's tests can't see. So no skinned control carries one: the chamfer is the `::before`'s (§R-B3), the ring and the glow lie inside the face, and `button-states.test.ts` asserts no `clip-path` and no `filter` on any skinned control in any state. jsdom draws nothing, so the look of the chamfered frame, the inside ring and the glow is his (SC-008).
- **The image's insets in a fixed row** (rework, S-6): drawn by `border-image-width` over the border and padding, never by `border-width`, so no content box shrinks; the frame's edge may lie under a tile's icon or words, a look for his eye (SC-008); the label's contrast over the band is measured by the round (§R-D4, Q74; rework 2).
- **The image's own transparency** (rework 2, R-3): the image is the `::before`'s, with the fallback's face colour clipped to the image's centre, so the transparent ring, corners, and glow show the glass, never a flat face. If a studio image's centre isn't opaque where the brief says it is, the face colour shows through it there; the judge measures the label only over centre pixels of alpha ≥ 0.5, so that is his look's.
- **Contrast over the room with clearer glass**: FR-005's rule raises one alpha in 5% steps if a ratio fails, recorded.
- **The round fails three times**: the CSS `metal` ships; the image skin stays `pending` and falls back; his page decides.
- **The studio needs a tool off the list**: the brief stops the round and hands back a note (as 021's brief does); a new tool is his to add.
