Summary: the room stops being code. Each kind of piece is a catalog entry, each placement and each selectable is a line of one scene file, and the compositor checks them and writes one room bundle (the intent's "composite game feed") for the 3D room, the 2D room, and the text copy. Studio models are plain; every readable word is drawn live from approved text; the laptop's charger shows whether the Author's machine is serving, and a neon "Langfuse" arrow counts the visitor's own cost.

### Edge Cases

- **A status answer arrives after the room was paused:** paused wins until resume (FR-041, row 2).
- **The relay answers 429** (visitors behind one address share the status bucket of 30 a minute): unknown, and the next request waits for `Retry-After` (FR-044).
- **The Author's own local-tier room**, whose `edge` answers 404 under `/api/`: every request fails, so it reads unknown, never plugged in (Assumptions, "No local status endpoint").
- **Two tabs:** two visits, two visit totals, two pollers; neither knows the other.
- **The dog sign:** the corpus has no dog yet, so the twin says it doesn't know until the Author writes it.
- **A design file that embeds a photograph:** it counts as a photo, and stays out of the studio's agent session.
- **Failover mid-visit:** a reply that says the fallback answered sets the tier to cloud until the next valid status answer (FR-041 row 7).
- **A screen too small for a wall tap** (a phone, a tablet, or the 2D room, wherever a side-wall board can't keep its own tap area): that board is chosen from the "In the room" list there, and tapping its picture selects nothing; on every screen where it keeps its tap area, it is tapped as any piece is (FR-064; T302).
- **A phone loses the 3D context under memory pressure:** the room restores it once, re-fetching pieces from the cache, or falls to 2D with the same pieces, states, and words (FR-058), and records the asset events.

## Requirements *(mandatory)*

### Functional Requirements

Terms used below. **Piece**: one kind of physical thing, described by a catalog entry. **Placement**: one placed instance of a piece in the scene file. **Selectable**: what a visitor clicks, with its member placements and a topic or an action. **Live face**: a flat area of a piece where the room draws (a screen, the slate, a sign's lettering area, the neon sign's word and counter panels). **Pose**: the placement a case of the laptop's switch shows (where the plug and lead lie). **Plain model**: a room model as FR-015 to FR-022 define it. **Fit check**: FR-015 to FR-019's checks and the budgets, run on one model by the compositor or the lookdev page (FR-068); a model fails its fit when it fails any of them, and the results are its fit findings. **Room text**: the words the room draws, in files approved by hash. **UI words**: the room's fixed interface words, reviewed like spec 002's `messages.yaml`. **Signal**: a read-only value the room displays: the tier, or a turn's cost. **The compositor**: the build step that checks the room's data and writes the room bundle. **The text copy**: the "Around the room" section, the drawn words and states as page text, visible to every visitor (FR-063). **The production framing**: the camera spec 002's FR-047 sets, on both reference stages. **The reference phone**: ADR 0010's (a Galaxy A54-class phone). **The reference desktop**: spec 002's (its FR-048), measured as a 1920 × 1200 window at a pixel ratio of 1, with the stage spec 002's layout gives it there. **A drafts build**: spec 002's `-drafts` images of dev and dev-cpu, and the self-contained drafts build of the vignette that FR-069 copies to the reference phone; **a release build**: any other. **The guard**: spec 003's task T005, as amended here.

**The room as data**

- **FR-001**: THE ROOM'S CONTENT SHALL be data, in these kinds: a piece catalog, one scene file (placements with their bindings, and selectables), topics, room text, UI words, looks, and fixtures. Each kind SHALL live in its own files, so a change to one kind touches no file of another.
- **FR-002**: EACH CATALOG ENTRY SHALL hold the piece's measured size, its stylized scale where FR-051 applies, its origin as a physical point from the dimension sheet with its expected bounds around that point, its named anchors, its live faces (position, size, facing, and pixel size), its glows, the signal value it claims if any, its stand-in shape, its 2D vector drawing, its budget, and its studio work order. WHERE a size came from an estimate rather than a measurement (the exception of 2026-10-22 among them), THE ENTRY SHALL mark it estimated, and its work order SHALL ask for the measurement.
- **FR-003**: EACH PLACEMENT SHALL mount on a named anchor of another placement, walls and floor included, with an offset and a turn, so that WHEN a parent moves, everything mounted on it moves with it. Only the room's shell SHALL have no mount.
- **FR-004**: EACH SELECTABLE SHALL have a permanent ID, a visible name, its member placements, and a topic or an action. Its ID SHALL be the usage event's ID and the conversation's feature. An ID SHALL never be reused for a different thing, and WHEN a selectable is removed, its ID SHALL leave the event list while its dashboard history stays. THE SCENE FILE SHALL list retired IDs, and THE COMPOSITOR SHALL refuse a piece, placement, selectable, or topic that takes one.
- **FR-005**: THE ROOM'S BEHAVIOUR KINDS SHALL be a closed list in code: mount, frame, live face, glow, switch, cable (a run drawn between two named anchors, with a sag, at its piece's stylized scale), and selectable (talk or open the resume); the renderers terminal, desktop session, neon sign, chalk, and sign; the signals tier and turn cost; and the look methods. The desktop session renderer draws a conversation column, with no logo. THE COMPOSITOR SHALL refuse any key its schema doesn't define and any value outside its declared type, and no data value SHALL be evaluated as code. A new kind SHALL be code, through the lifecycle.
- **FR-006**: WHEN a piece is moved, added, removed, regrouped, retargeted, or reworded, or a switch gains a case for a value its signal already has, THE CHANGE SHALL be data only. A test SHALL make each of these changes on a copy of the data and pass with no source file changed.
- **FR-007**: IF the room's code holds an ID that only data defines (a piece, placement, selectable, or topic), THEN A SOURCE SCAN SHALL fail `make check`, naming the file and the ID.
- **FR-008**: THE ROOM SHALL keep spec 002's hand-off and its paper as code, taking only their anchors (the twin's hand and the visitor's) from data.
- **FR-078**: THE DEFAULT SCENE SHALL lay the work area out as the Author laid it out on 2026-10-23 and 2026-10-26 (Clarifications):
  - the room's shell, a brick cube 7 ft wide, 7 ft high, and 4 ft deep (2.1336 × 2.1336 × 1.2192 m), open at the front with no ceiling, on a cement floor, seen straight into its open front;

  - the chalkboard "on a brick wall that is left of the monitor", read as the left side wall, hung high there, and the snowboard hung low on the same wall, next to it, with small gaps at the wall's corners (D1);
  - the bicycle on the right side wall, next to the laptop;
  - the twin a little right of the desk, by the laptop; and the visitor, whom the Author put "in middle, being able to look at everything", still central, but nearer the open front and a little left, so the monitor, the sign, and the laptop stay in view (D3).

  WHERE a position is estimated rather than measured, THE SCENE SHALL mark it so, as FR-002 does for sizes.

  | # | Condition | Tier |
  |---|---|---|
  | 1 | the scene's live source is off | not-live |
  | 2 | the room is paused, or the page is hidden | paused, which counts as unknown |
  | 3 | no completed request yet; or the last request failed, took over 5 s, answered other than 200, or lacked any of the three fields | unknown |

  | 9 | `state` `"normal"`, `local_tier` `"down"` | cloud |
  | 10 | anything else | unknown |

### Key Entities

Terms (above) define most entities; FR-002 to FR-004, FR-041, and FR-043 list the rest. These are the ones no FR spells out:

- **Work order**: the generated, committed brief for one piece: method, shots, measurements, origin, live faces, states, budget, the look's material notes, export rules, and the record's template.
